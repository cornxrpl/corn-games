Menu

  1. Choose how to play. Practice lets you choose 1–50 kicks and Easy or Hard. Competitive gives you five kicks per round.
  2. Climb the leagues. Competitive goals stay banked across rounds. The first four leagues need five goals each; after Pro, unlock the World Cup League and bank ten goals there.
  3. Aim inside the goal. Tap or click the spot where you want the ball to go. Shots too close to the frame miss.
  4. Earn coins. Score 100% for 25 coins, 80% or higher for 10, or 50% or higher for 5. League wins add 500, 1,000, 1,500, 2,000, then a 250,000-coin World Cup bonus.
  5. Build your collection. Buy each tier in order: vehicles and homes, then islands, countries, continents, and finally Planet Earth. New purchases appear in this menu.
