HOW TO PLAY

Run through the farm fields as Corn Man or XRPMan. Stomp the critters, dodge the tractors, and grab every ear of corn you can.

  • Move: arrow keys / WASD, or the on-screen buttons.
  • Jump: Space, ↑, or the A button. Hold longer to jump higher. Stomp enemies from above.
  • Power-up crates (the ones with a corn mark): bump one from below and a power-up rises up and sits on top of the crate — jump up and walk into it to grab it. They come in a set order: first 🥾 the Speed Boot (run faster), then 🌽 the Corn Thrower (throw corn at critters and tractors with X / the B button), then 🌽 x2 Double Corn (throw twice as much corn at once). You keep them for the rest of the run. Every field — the Combine's arena too — has at least 3 power-up crates spread along it, so you can earn the whole set in a single level.
  • Golden corn makes you GOLDEN: one free hit, you can smash bricks, and you can throw kernels too.
  • Level goal: reach the giant ear of corn on the tall stalk at the end of the field. Then it's inside the farmhouse for a home-cooked meal — every meal earns back a heart.
  • Lives & corn: you start with 3 lives. Every 100 corn you collect earns an extra life, and the corn counter rolls back to 0 and starts counting toward the next one. The corn counter only resets on a Game Over — finishing a level or losing a life keeps your count.
  • Energy: each life has an energy bar by the hearts. A hit from a critter, the Barn Monster, or a hay bale drains one chunk — 4 hits empty the bar and cost the life, and the next life starts with a full bar. Falling in a pit or running out of time still costs the whole life at once. Lose a life and you respawn right where it ended — on the last safe ground at the edge of the hole for a pit fall — with a full energy bar and a moment of mercy invincibility. Score, corn, power-ups, monster damage, and the level itself are all kept; the field only starts over on a Game Over.
  • The Barn Monster: a big monster guards the end of every field, just before the finish stalk (every field except the Combine's). It takes 13 thrown-corn hits to beat — the counter above its head shows how many are left — and it lobs hay bales at you, so keep moving. No corn thrower yet? It can't block you: just jump clean over it and finish the level.
  • Tester cheat: on the title screen, tap the word SUPER 5 times to turn on invincibility. A ★ INVINCIBLE tag shows in the stats while it's on.
  • Tester cheat — level select: during the game, tap SCORE in the stats at the top 5 times to open the level select. (The title screen shows no stats, so this one is in-game only.) Enter any level from 1 to 40 and hit GO to start a fresh run there, or CANCEL to go back to where you were, untouched.
  • Tester cheat — farmhouse jump: on the title screen, tap the 🏆 on the right of "Bros." 5 times to jump straight to the farmhouse at the end of the level — the home-cooked meal, the +1 heart, and then on to the next level, just like finishing the field. If a run is already going, it's that level's farmhouse; if not, a fresh run starts (with your picked hero, or Corn Man) and it's Level 1's farmhouse. The first 4 taps do nothing.
  • Tester cheat — Level 37 jump: on the title screen, tap the combine harvester in the title art 5 times to jump straight to Level 37 as a fresh run — score, corn, lives, and power-ups all reset, with your picked hero (or Corn Man if you haven't picked one). The first 4 taps do nothing.
  • Tester cheat — moon jump: during the game, tap TIME in the stats at the top 5 times to jump straight to the moon — Cookie's level — as a fresh run with your picked hero (score, corn, lives, and power-ups all reset). Up there, collect 20 of the 25 moon rocks to open Cookie's gate, grab the 3 power-up crates so you can throw corn, and beat Cookie with 13 thrown corns — watch out, he throws cookies back. The first 4 taps do nothing.
  • Corn Warp: some fields hide a golden silo. Stand at its door and press ↓ to warp ahead 3 levels.
  • The giant corn stalk (Level 2): bump the first hay bale of Level 2 from below and a huge corn stalk grows out of it, beanstalk-style, up past the top of the screen. Grab on and hold ↑ to climb it (tap ◀ or ▶ to hop off, ↓ climbs back down). Up past the top are the Corn Warps: three golden silos that warp your run — score, corn, lives, and power-ups all kept — to Level 10, Level 20, or Level 30. Changed your mind? The wooden door up there takes you back down to Level 2, right at the stalk.
  • The giant corn stalk (Level 37): Level 37 hides the same trick — bump the first hay bale of Level 37 from below, climb the stalk that grows out of it, and the Corn Warps up top offer two warps this time: Level 40, the Corporate Combine boss arena, or Secret Level 589, Stone's field. The wooden door takes you back down to Level 37, right at the stalk.
  • Level 40: the Corporate Combine boss. The arena floor is clear end to end now, so it can charge straight across — climb the low hay stacks at either end onto the high hayloft and pelt it with kernels as it rolls underneath you. On the left, three single hay bales stair-step up from the wall stack, each one higher than the last, to a high perch above the loft — a perfect sniping spot. It also fires hay bales and corn at you: the hay bales arc like the Barn Monster's, and its corn is a dark-red horizontal attack cob with a red glow — not a pickup, so don't run into it. Dodge or jump over both — touching a fired hay bale or a fired corn cob drains a chunk of energy, just like any other hit. And beating the first Combine isn't the end: the screen flashes and the SUPER COMBINE rolls in — twice as big, with a full health bar of its own, throwing 3 hay bales and 3 corn cobs at a time. It's so tall it smashes through the hayloft as it rolls — don't stand still up there! Beat that one too and the farm is saved.

WATCH OUT FOR

  • 🐔 Chickens and 🐷 pigs wander the rows. 🐄 Cows are wide. 🚜 Tractors roll fast and don't stop for fences.
  • Pits between the fields: fall in and you lose a heart.
  • The clock: run out of time and you lose a heart too.
