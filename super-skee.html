How to Play

  • You get 8 balls each round.
  • Aim and Power slide back and forth on their own, like the football game — Aim sweeps left to right and back, Power sweeps soft to hard and back.
  • Tap STOP on the Aim meter (or tap anywhere on the Aim row) to stop it — where it stops is your angle for the throw. It stays stopped while Power keeps moving.
  • Tap STOP on the Power meter (or tap anywhere on the Power row) to stop it — where it stops is your power for the throw. Around 72 power lands near the middle rings — too soft falls short, too hard flies past.
  • Once both meters are stopped, ROLL BALL lights up — press it and the ball rolls with exactly the angle and power you stopped on. The Space bar also works: first press stops Aim, second stops Power, third rolls.
  • Rings score 10 · 20 · 30 · 40 · 50, and the very center is 100.
  • The little colour dot on the Aim and Power bars shows what a roll with the current Aim + Power would score — watch it change as the meters sweep and time your STOPs by colour: green = 100 · orange = 50 · yellow = 40 · blue = 30 · black = 20 · white = 10 · red = 0.
  • You earn 1 ticket for every 50 points. At the end of the round your tickets print out — tap Collect to bank them.
  • Every round is a level — finish a round and the next round is the next level, all the way up to Level 100 (after Level 100 it wraps back to Level 1).
  • Every 10th level (10, 20, 30 … 100) is a bonus level: a pop-up says tickets are worth 1000x, and they are — earn enough points for 1 ticket and you bank 1,000 tickets, earn 2 and you bank 2,000, and so on.
  • After you collect, choose Game Over to go back to the home screen, or Play Again for a fresh 8 balls and a fresh score — your banked tickets stay banked either way.
  • Spend banked tickets in the Prize Shop — tap the big PRIZE SHOP button under the game to open it. All the prizes live in the shop. Redeemed prizes stay yours until you tap RESET PRIZES in the shop — that clears every owned prize (tickets are not refunded) so you can collect them again.
  • Own 1 of every prize in the shop and the screen blinks and flashes — the 🏎️ Sports Car grand prize unlocks for 40,000 tickets. Buy it to win the game!
