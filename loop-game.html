LOOP GAME

The jungle path never ends. The monsters never stop. And the clock… never reaches zero.

Your timer starts at 30 seconds. At 5, the timer lights up — tap it to grab +25 more seconds. Miss the tap and the loop adds them anyway, forever.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Run! Your runner flees on his own — your job is to keep him on his feet.
  • Jump with SPACE, ↑, a tap on the jungle, or the green JUMP button to clear logs, rocks, stumps, toadstools, coconut piles, vine tangles and puddles.
  • Need distance? Hit the orange RUN button (or press R) for a burst of speed that pulls you away from the monsters.
  • Watch the vines! Snakes hang from the vines up top. When one shakes, hisses and flashes !, it's about to drop and strike low — jump its head or sprint past before it lands. Then it slithers back up… and can drop again. A strike only makes you stumble, same as any obstacle.
  • Stumble and they gain. Every crash lets the monsters creep closer. Run clean and they fall behind.
  • They can never catch you. When a monster gets extremely close, it slows right down and stalls at your heels — terrifying, but it can never grab you. The clock is the only enemy… and it never reaches zero.
  • The timer: 30 seconds… down to 5… the timer lights up — TAP THE TIMER to grab +25 seconds (jingle included!). Too slow? The loop adds the +25 anyway. There is no zero. There is only the loop.
  • Weather: every time the timer resets — each +25 and each fresh run — the jungle weather changes at random: sunny, cloudy, rainy, stormy with lightning, sunset, or night with a moon. It's just the sky putting on a show; the run itself never changes.
  • 🏰 The castle snake: the timer hit :02, you ran through the castle door, and it slammed behind you — but the loop has one last guard: a giant two-headed striking snake! Pull out your slingshot and fire rocks with the big FIRE button, a tap right on the snake, or SPACE. Land 50 rock hits to beat it and win the game! When both heads rear back, hiss and flash !, it's about to strike — hit DODGE or move with ◀ ▶. Both heads lunge together as one strike — a strike that lands costs 1 energy from your castle energy bar — you only have 5. Lose them all and it's Game Over — tap Start Over to go back to the Home Screen and try again.
  • Careful with the +25 tap: if you are headed for the castle and you tap the timer for +25 at 5 seconds, the castle waits — the door appears when the timer next counts down to :02.
