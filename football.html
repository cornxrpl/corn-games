How to play

  1. Tap once to stop the moving power meter near the center.
  2. Tap again to stop the accuracy meter dead center.
  3. Read the wind. Every make moves you back 5 yards.
  4. Competitive mode keeps a running total of every kick through all four leagues. Make 5 field goals to advance; total kicks reset only when you replay after the Super Bowl, reset to High School, or return Home.
  5. Each new league starts farther back, with a smaller green target and a faster meter.
  6. In Pro and the Super Bowl, both power and accuracy must land in the green section or the kick will miss.
  7. Your offensive line clashes with the defensive rush as you kick.
  8. Perfect kicks earn bonus coins and a bigger celebration.
